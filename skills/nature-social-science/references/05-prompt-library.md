## Step 1 — Interest Mapper

**When to use:** An interest, broad topic, or tentative question needs bounded exploration.

**Required input:** Entry mode; original interest/topic/RQ; phenomenon, actors or materials; setting/time/scale; known concepts; exclusions; constraints and largest unknown.

**Stop condition:** Complete the one-page Research Search Space and Step 1 gate, then stop before later stages.

**What to save:** Save the named output, evidence levels/states, failures, unknowns, reasons, and next decisive action. Update Current Research State.

```text
Use Interest Mapper for Step 1 of my research-topic workflow.

Current input:
[Paste the required inputs listed above; mark unknown fields unknown.]

Task:
Identify Mode A/B/C. Disentangle core concepts, expand adjacent vocabulary, expose assumptions about direction, causality, values, and methods, and offer alternative boundaries. Preserve at least two possible directions. Explain how constraints affect scope. Record distinct meanings, inclusion/exclusion, undecided items, and one decisive next check.
Do not generate candidate questions, titles, or hypotheses; assert novelty or a research gap; or let a convenient dataset or preferred method choose scope. This stage normally needs no large search. Any terminology lookup must retain source level and uncertainty.

Evidence and conduct:
Do not fabricate citations, sources, or results. Record unknowns without guessing. Seek contradictions, null results, reverse relationships, and alternative explanations where relevant. Do not assume a preferred method or turn personal interest into proven importance. No result in a bounded search does not establish absence of research. Give stable links or DOIs for consequential sources and state METADATA ONLY / ABSTRACT LEVEL / FULL-TEXT LEVEL / PRIMARY/OFFICIAL.
```
