Carry only valid summaries and designated worksheets. Full chats, search logs, old versions, and discarded options remain in their original records. The eight steps must not become an endlessly growing conversation.

## Entry → Step 1

**Carry forward:** Entry Diagnostic, mode/reason, original interest/topic/RQ, known hard constraints, and assumptions open to challenge.

**Leave behind:** Artificially polished questions created to qualify as Mode C, lengthy dataset/method descriptions, and personal history irrelevant to entry.

**Human decision:** Choose the entry mode and which practical/personal boundaries apply.

**Blocking unknown:** No sustained interest, phenomenon, process, or material can be described, or non-negotiable ethical/practical boundaries are wholly unknown.

## Step 1 → Step 2

**Carry forward:** One-page Search Space; concepts that must remain distinct; actors/materials/context/time/scale; adjacent terms; exclusions; Step 1 gate; one unresolved boundary.

**Leave behind:** Exhaustive concept brainstorming, excluded boundaries, prematurely generated questions/hypotheses, and irrelevant chat.

**Human decision:** Confirm the territory remains worth exploring, accepting that evidence may weaken the initial view.

**Blocking unknown:** Core terms remain conflated or scope cannot support bounded searching.

## Step 2 → Step 3

**Carry forward:** Landscape Map; problem/theory/mechanism families and debates; robust findings and limits; contradictions, nulls, reversals, negative cases, alternatives; at most five decisive sources and levels; search bounds/failures; gate.

**Leave behind:** One-paper summaries, full results lists, duplicate citations, backend logs, and marginal materials that do not change the map.

**Human decision:** Identify the scholarly conversation worth pursuing and initial assumptions no longer defensible.

**Blocking unknown:** Only keywords rather than a conversation, or decisive structural judgements rest on insufficiently checked abstracts.

## Step 3 → Step 4

**Carry forward:** Prioritised Problem Cards; known/unresolved/consequence/distinguishing-evidence answers; closest work and level; alternatives, objections, terminating conditions, unknowns, and gate.

**Leave behind:** Rejected thin-coverage claims, premature final questions, and landscape material unrelated to selected problems.

**Human decision:** Choose the few problems worth testing and explain the understanding their answers would change.

**Blocking unknown:** Necessary observations cannot be named or evidence distinguishing competing explanations cannot be imagined.

## Step 4 → Step 5

**Carry forward:** Prioritised problems; 1–3 surviving evidence routes and actual states; construct/meaning, unit, timing, coverage, access, ethics; claim ceilings; bias, unknowns, stop conditions; gate.

**Leave behind:** Mismatched famous datasets, full catalogues/codebooks/variable IDs/archive/API inventories, convenient proxies unable to observe the core problem, and unverified access promises.

**Human decision:** Decide acceptable evidential compromises and whether the narrower claim remains worthwhile.

**Blocking unknown:** No route observes the core problem, access/ethics dependencies are unmanageable, or states exceed their basis.

## Step 5 → Step 6

**Carry forward:** Usually 6–12 Candidate RQ Cards; three generation routes and failures; substantive differences; evidence states, mechanisms, competitors, claim ceilings, contribution hypotheses, likely fatal flaws; gate.

**Leave behind:** Merged location/sample/material/method variants, generator preferences/rankings, promotional contribution prose, and whole Steps 1–4 chats.

**Human decision:** Confirm genuinely different knowledge tasks and permit rejection of the original question.

**Blocking unknown:** Candidates remain topics or lack a knowledge problem, evidence route, and claim ceiling.

## Step 6 → Step 7

**Carry forward:** SURVIVE candidates and completed explicit redesigns; Novelty Audits; closest work and levels; strongest objections, counterevidence, assumptions, claim ceilings; disagreements, dispositions, and gate.

**Leave behind:** Promotional rejected-candidate prose, parked candidates without decisive evidence, repetitive minor criticisms, scores, and generator preferences. Keep rejection reasons in their records.

**Human decision:** Judge whether the strongest objection has actually been answered and whether the claimed difference matters in the discipline.

**Blocking unknown:** Closest work carrying a novelty/rejection judgement remains insufficiently checked, or construct/evidence fit remains undecided.

## Step 7 → Step 8

**Carry forward:** Dossiers disposed PROCEED TO COMPARISON; eight hard-gate states and bases; minimum study and lawful reality check; decisive twelve-month dependencies; training/supervision/resources/personal bounds; fatal failure conditions; gate.

**Leave behind:** Candidates with untreated FAIL or current PARK status, routine month-by-month to-dos, secondary training details, technical implementation, scores, weights, and ranks.

**Human decision:** Confirm that each minimum study remains worthwhile and accept its actual learning, risk, and life burden.

**Blocking unknown:** Decisive access, ethics, safety, timing, skills, supervision, or information-value UNKNOWN lacks a realistic verification route, or FAIL remains untreated.

## Step 8 → Supervisor/Application Discussion

**Carry forward:** At most three Topic Cards; written, unscored comparison; greatest risks and fatal failure conditions; supervision/access/resource/ethics confirmations still needed; one decisive action per direction; gate.

**Leave behind:** Full conversations, backend logs, long rejected-candidate prose, automatic winners or success predictions, and unverified promises. Preserve supporting records separately.

**Human decision:** Choose which trade-offs to discuss, which uncertainties to disclose, and when to choose. Multiple directions may remain active; AI does not make the final commitment.

**Blocking unknown:** Finalists' substantive differences cannot be explained, cards hide hard risks, or decisive supervision/institutional/access judgements remain unavailable.

A shortlist supports a serious discussion, not an admission or funding forecast. Application prose may be provisional while explicitly stating the knowledge problem, significance, evidential ceiling, dependencies, and fallback. Do not imply permission or supervision has been granted.

## Resume rule

Open `CURRENT_RESEARCH_STATE.md`, inspect CURRENT GATE and MOST IMPORTANT UNKNOWN, then load only the matching worksheet and prompt. Recheck changeable access rules, dates, sources, or institutional facts. Execute NEXT VERIFICATION ACTION rather than replaying the conversation from the beginning.

Update only fields affected by new evidence. Changed literature, access, ethics advice, or trial results can reopen an earlier PASS. Preserve parked/rejected records and their explicit reopening conditions.
