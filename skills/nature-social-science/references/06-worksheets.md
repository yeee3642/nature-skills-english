## 1. Entry Diagnostic

Use before the workflow, or when changing direction completely. Decide whether the starting point is an interest, broad topic, or tentative question.

```text

Can I identify possible evidence: fully / partly / no

Reason:
Assumptions open to challenge or rejection:
Step 1 approach: full exploration / targeted audit

```
