# Gate 3 — Auto-Validation Checklist

<!-- English edition: modified 2026-10-20; see EDITION-NOTICE.md at package root. -->

Apply relevant checks after drafting. Mark pass, missing-but-repairable, or
structural failure, and identify the actual missing evidence. Do not claim that a
textual checklist proves experimental reproducibility or factual correctness.

## Citations

- Each externally sourced factual claim has a traceable citation.
- Every citation resolves to an entry and every listed reference is used.
- Important numbers and parameters have sources or are explicitly proposed values.

## Methods

- Materials/reagents have supplier, purity, and batch where relevant and known.
- Equipment models and critical parameters are identified.
- Temperature, duration, atmosphere, pressure, and other essential conditions are supplied.
- Replication, statistical methods, and exclusion criteria are explicit when applicable.
- Missing details remain marked rather than invented.

## Terms and logic

- Abbreviations and specialized terms are defined at first use or sourced.
- Variables and symbols are explained.
- The discussion addresses the stated question.
- Each objective or hypothesis has a corresponding planned or completed test.
- Conclusions remain within the evidence; novelty claims have an appropriate search basis.

## Displays and formatting

- Figure/table numbering is continuous and cross-references resolve.
- Displays have captions; axes have labels and units; legends are legible.
- Reference style, numerical precision, and units are consistent.
- Chemical subscripts and superscripts are correct, for example MgCl2 rendered with subscripts in the final format.

## Language

Check spelling, grammar, clear subjects, and tense appropriate to the section and
whether work is proposed or completed. Check overly nested modifiers and
redundancy. Apply additional language-specific checks only for an explicitly
requested translation.

## Decision

A pass requires all applicable critical checks. A repairable omission gets a
warning with the missing item. A structural omission fails the gate and returns
to the relevant foundation, evidence, or section step rather than another round
of cosmetic polishing.
