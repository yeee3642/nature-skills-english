# Boundaries and intake (Step 1)

<!-- English edition: modified 2026-10-20; see EDITION-NOTICE.md at package root. -->

Before analysing patent points, confirm only missing facts that affect the work: the technical subject or product module, the intended claim category (method, system, apparatus, or undecided), and whether technical contact details should be included. Reuse facts already supplied. Contact names, telephone numbers, and email addresses remain `[TO CONFIRM]` unless provided; never infer them.

Information gaps need not block source analysis. Record provisional assumptions separately from established facts, summarize the confirmed scope, and continue with `project_scan.md`. Establish the target jurisdiction before applying any jurisdiction-specific formal drafting rules. Generic technical disclosures are drafted in English by default.
