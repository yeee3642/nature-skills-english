# Candidate patent points and synthesis (Steps 3-4)

<!-- English edition: modified 2026-10-20; see EDITION-NOTICE.md at package root. -->

Identify three to five supported candidate technical contributions. For each, explain the technical context, distinguishing mechanism, preliminary difference from known approaches, implementation evidence, and open questions. A plausible extrapolation is not an established implementation and must be labelled as a proposal.

Check whether related mechanisms form a coherent method-and-system concept rather than an arbitrary collection. Assess technical completeness, realistic scope, and the feature combination's interaction. Revisit differentiation after the prior-art search; a search does not establish patentability or entitlement.

Default to the strongest single disclosure. If the user requests several, distinguish their core contributions and establish a writing order. Do not infer inventorship, ownership, novelty, or legal sufficiency from a paper's contribution list.
