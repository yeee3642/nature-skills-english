# Corrections to an existing disclosure

<!-- English edition: modified 2026-10-20; see EDITION-NOTICE.md at package root. -->

Read `iteration_context.md`, the baseline draft, and the user's correction. Identify the affected chapter, original issue, and supported replacement. Repair facts and implementation logic in Chapter 3 and related embodiments/parameters. For notation errors, synchronize the 3.4.1 definitions, equations, 3.5 symbol column, and Chapter 6 examples. For prior-art errors, repair Chapter 1 and search again when necessary. For protection-point wording, align Chapters 4-5 with Chapter 3.

Avoid rewriting unrelated sections. Save the complete corrected draft as new timestamped Markdown and matching Word files, preserve prior versions, run checks 8.2/8.3, and append a `correct` record to `disclosure_revision_log.md`.

Deliver paths and a concise English Correction summary covering changes, evidence, and implications for protection points or search. Any subsequent claim-emphasis suggestion stays outside the disclosure and follows its existing evidence. Do not require the user to say a fixed iteration keyword or restart patent mining without a request.
